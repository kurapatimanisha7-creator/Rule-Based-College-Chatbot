# Week 5 - Conversation State and Working Conversation Loop

## Project Title

**Rule-Based College Chatbot**

## Overview

Week 5 focuses on improving the college chatbot by implementing **conversation state management** and a **working conversation loop**.

In the previous weeks, the chatbot mainly detected the user's intent and provided an appropriate answer. In Week 5, the chatbot is extended to maintain the current conversation state, store conversation history, and handle simple follow-up questions.

This makes the chatbot more interactive and capable of maintaining a continuous conversation with the user.

---

## Learning Objective

The main objective of Week 5 is to understand and implement:

- Conversation State
- Conversation History
- Context Handling
- Working Conversation Loop
- State Reset
- Continuous User-Chatbot Interaction
